## Video Explanation

### 1. HR Analysis Overview
Explains the overall HR metrics, employee attributes, and department-wise retention and attrition analysis.

### 2. HR Executive Dashboard
Demonstrates KPI cards, department-wise HR summary, attrition by department, age band, and overtime.

### 3. Employee Engagement Analysis
Explains Environment Satisfaction, Job Satisfaction, Relationship Satisfaction, Work-Life Balance, and Average Engagement Score.

### 4. Workforce and Compensation Analysis
Demonstrates department-wise employee distribution, salary bands, gender distribution, average monthly income by job role, years at company, and business travel.

### 5. Performance and Promotion Analysis
Explains promotion eligibility, department-wise promotion analysis, promotion rate, years since last promotion, and promotion eligibility by job level.

### 6. Retrenchment Analysis
Demonstrates retrenchment-risk KPIs, department analysis, job-role analysis, business travel analysis, and the decomposition tree.

### 7. Employee Risk Analysis
Explains the rule-based employee risk score, risk categories, high-risk employee count, risk distribution, and employee risk details.

### 8. Raw Employee Data
Demonstrates the employee-level data table and available employee attributes.

### 9. Employee Detail – Drill-Through Functionality
Demonstrates how to right-click an employee record in Raw Data, select Drill through, open the Employee Detail page, view employee information, and return using the Back button.

### 10. Interactive Features
Demonstrates bookmark navigation, slicers, Clear Filters, Reset Dashboard, and drill-through functionality.

## Project Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI

## Project Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of Power BI for HR data analysis through data transformation, DAX calculations, KPI development, interactive visualizations, and employee-level drill-through analysis.
